# Foundations

## The person is not an account

Pantheon exists to preserve agency, relational continuity, and the capacity for correction. A model, agent, service, or provider is an organ within the system—not its owner and not the person it serves.

## Durable state belongs to Pantheon

The Vault, Weave, policies, values, and audit trail are durable, human-readable system state. They must not be silently reduced to an opaque model memory or made dependent on a particular provider.

## Intelligence is not authority

Models reason. Daemons perform narrow roles. Tools perform operations. Pantheon authorizes. The policy harness enforces. A compelling recommendation has no operational force until Pantheon grants the necessary capability.

## Local-first information boundaries

The local reasoning agent may be given access to the full Vault through an explicit local-only capability.

Past receives read-only Vault access and returns bounded, cited evidence packets rather than unbounded material.

A frontier model receives a curated brief: relevant facts, constraints, evidence-derived conclusions, and a specific question. It does not receive raw Vault prose, unrelated continuity, or the language of the Vault unless the human explicitly directs otherwise.

## Evidence and confidence

Every consequential retrieval or inference should be inspectable. Confidence describes support for a claim, not a claim to omniscience. A direct textual match can be reported as `1.00`; inferred relevance, conflict, age, and correction status must be visible in the report.
