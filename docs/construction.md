# Construction

Pantheon is assembled as a system of constrained, replaceable organs around durable human continuity. Construction proceeds from boundaries outward: first what belongs to the person, then what the runtime may authorize, then what a daemon, model, or tool may do.

## Construction order

1. Preserve durable state in inspectable local forms.
2. Define a narrow role and capability surface for each daemon.
3. Build deterministic routing and policy enforcement around those roles.
4. Attach local reasoning before frontier reasoning.
5. Add external execution only behind explicit capabilities and audit records.
6. Measure outcomes, corrections, and reportbacks before automating further.

## The Fates

Present receives what is happening now and forms accountable observations.

Past retrieves relevant, cited continuity from the Vault. It returns two to five ranked jumps when evidence exists; it does not rewrite its source material.

Future develops trajectories from Present's situation and Past's evidence. It proposes instructions; it does not authorize execution.

Pantheon is the caller and orchestrator. It grants bounded authority, invokes the appropriate deity, and records what occurred.

## Capability rule

No component inherits authority merely because another component has it. A request must name or receive only the capability required for its task. Any component can be replaced without losing the person's Vault, Weave, values, or audit history.

## Correction rule

The system must preserve correction history. A correction does not make the earlier record disappear; it marks it as struck or superseded and identifies why. Stronger automation is never a substitute for the human capacity to inspect, correct, revoke, and leave.
